Suikendo or way of the water fist combines movement, defense, and offensive movements to deliver maximum force with simultaneous timing.
Much much more could be written but these comprise the basic components:


Movements:
  • Backswims
  • Traps
  • Expansion
  • Compression
  • Lays

Suikendo Patterns:
  1. Eagle and Compression
  2. Eagle, Compression, Break
  3. Eagle, Compression, Inside Triple Hit
  4. Eagle, Compression, Outside Double and Break
  5. Eagle, Compression, Dropping
  6. Eagle, Compression, Windmill

Suikendo Complexes:
  • Suikendo Complex
  • Suikendo Dai Ni
  • Suikendo Seminar Complex (an "Eight Second" complex)
