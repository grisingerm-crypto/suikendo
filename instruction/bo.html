Bo Curriculum:


Hojoundo (Supplementary Training):
  • Dai Ichi
    1. Jodan Uchi: vertical strike to top of head
    2. Jodan Naname Uchi: diagonal strike to the head or neck
    3. Chudan Naname Uchi: middle section side strike
    4. Gedan Naname Uchi: side strike to the knee
    5. Chudan Kaku Uke and Tsuki: middle block and thrust to throat
  • Dai Ni
    1. Gedan Harai Uke and Jodan Naname Uchi: Low inside sweeping block and diagonal head strike
    2. Gedan Hane Uke and Gedan Nukibo: Reverse low block from hip and spear thrust to arch of foot
    3. Sunakake: sand throw to eyes
    4. Gedan Osae Uke: Reverse circular low block
    5. Gyaku Uchi and Gyaku Tsuki: Upper strike to jaw and move forward with reverse grip thrust to throat
  • Dai San
    1. Gedan Yoko Uke and Jodan Naname Uchi: Outside low block (reverse grip) and diagonal head strike
    2. Jodan Naname Uchi and Tsuki: Diagonal head strike and thrust to throat
    3. Chudan Nagashi Uchi, Gedan Uke and Jodan Naname Uchi: Upper angle side strike to ribs, reverse grip low outside block, and move forward with diagonal head strike
    4. Gedan Yoko Uke, Gedan Osae Uke and Jodan Naname Uchi: Outside low block, circular low block, and move forward with diagonal head strike
    5. Gorenda: 5 attacks combination: Diagonal head strike, upper sweep strike to jaw, down strike to collarbone, middle side strike and diagonal head strike
