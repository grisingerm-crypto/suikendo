Bunkai are 'kata in application.' They don't represent the only expression of the kata into a usable form, but present a common usage for the techniques of the kata in a two-person form.
Students applying Bunkai learn focus with power and control and refine proper footwork and distance relative to the uke, or attacker:


Pinan Bunkai:
  • Pinan Eedan Bunkai
  • Pinan Shodan Bunkai - Attacking Side
  • Pinan Shodan Bunkai - Defending Side
  • Pinan Sandan Bunkai - Attacking Side
  • Pinan Sandan Bunkai - Defending Side
  • Pinan Sandan Bunkai
  • Pinan Sandan Bunkai - Opposite Roles
  • Pinan Yodan Bunkai
  • Pinan Godan Bunkai

Naihanshi Bunkai:
  • Naihanshi Shodan Bunkai
  • Naihanshi Shodan Bunkai - Alternate

Complexes are similar to Bunkai and comprise sets of movements corresponding to several kata or a common set of actions:
  • Pinan Complex
  • Naihanshi Complex
  • Breaking Complex
  • Yakasoku Complex

The Suikendo Complex is listed with other Suikendo material.
