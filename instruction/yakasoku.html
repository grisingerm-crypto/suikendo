Yakasoku are divided into three sets:


Set One:
  1. High Blocks and Counter
  2. Middle Blocks and Counter
  3. Low Blocks and Counter
  4. Inside Blocks and Counter

Set Two:
  1. Sidestep Inside and Counter
  2. Sidestep Outside and Counter
  3. High/Low Blocks and Counter
  4. Inside/Elbow Blocks and Counter
  5. High Block, Sidestep Inside and Counter
  6. Gate Inside
  7. Gate Outside
  8. Inside Spin, Three Strikes
  9. Outside Spin, Break
  10. Intercept
  11. Backswim, Double Hit
  12. Eagle, Strike and Hit

Set Three:
  1. Two Punch, Sidestep Outside and Counter
  2. Two Punch, High Block and Sudo
  3. High Middle Low, High Middle Low Kick
  4. Four Blocks and Counter
  5. Two Punch, Low High Block and Break
